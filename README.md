# HoopTapes

HoopTapes is an NBA and college-basketball alert and highlight platform: it ingests live scores and play-by-play, detects close games and viral moments (big win-probability swings, scoring runs, buzzer-beaters), renders a highlight card for each moment, and sends fans alerts and post-game highlight packs by push and email, each linking out to the official highlights. It's built from scratch as an SRE/DevOps capstone, so the app stays small and the platform around it (Terraform on AWS, CI/CD with OIDC, ECS and EKS, GitOps, SLOs and observability) is the serious part. HoopTapes is an unofficial fan project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NBA, the NCAA, any team, player, or broadcaster, and all trademarks and footage belong to their owners.
